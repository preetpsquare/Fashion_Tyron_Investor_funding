FASHION TRYON | Investor discussion notes | Revised 19 September 2026 All amounts USD. Founder ambition, research and modeled requirements are distinguished.

THE NEXT ROUND FOR THE GROWTH PLAN Recommend a $1.5m seed planning target, subject to launch evidence. The original $200k founding capital remains unchanged.

WHY $1.5M? The target model's maximum cumulative funding gap after the founding round is approximately $1,229,248, including two-month seller collection lags. A $1.5m round leaves about $270,752 above that mathematical minimum. If revenue is slower, the requirement rises.

Use of funds is governed by the monthly model, not an arbitrary percentage split. The first 18 funded months (February 2027–July 2028) plan approximately $1,044,470 of listed spending. The remaining capital and collections support the later growth ramp and payment delays. Avoid describing this as 18 months of guaranteed runway regardless of results.

FIRST 18 FUNDED MONTHS — PLANNING ALLOWANCES Category | Amount Paid acquisition | $330,000 Organic programs | $45,000 Fixed team, product, partnerships and administration | $502,500 Variable support, catalog and operations | $155,870 Seller servicing | $11,100 Unallocated portion of $1.5m for subsequent ramp/reserve | $455,530

WHAT THE PROJECTIONS MUST PROVE 1. Launch the useful iOS/Android styling and shopping journey; target 5,000 active US users by January 2027. 2. People share outfits or styling experiences and bring genuinely new people who activate. Measure organic origin and deduplicate paid exposure. 3. Measure new organic activations per prior active user per month. The plan needs about 29 in every 100 in Year 1's post-launch period, tapering to about 15 per 100 in Year 5. 4. Prove repeat use. The model assumes monthly active continuation rising from 85% to 95%; this is not yet achieved. 5. Verify shopping activity and net commissions. Free wardrobe usage can drive engagement without purchases; measure both separately. 6. Sell sponsorship and tools based on actual audience and seller value. Year 5 assumes 200 paying sponsor accounts and 200 paid tools accounts, not contracts already won. 7. Measure costs as the audience grows. Organic distribution can lower blended acquisition cost, but support, product work and infrastructure still need funding.

THE REVENUE DESTINATION The worked Year 5 target is $101.439m affiliate + $12m sponsorship + $1.2m tools = $114.639m platform revenue. It needs 8.5m average monthly actives and 13.760m year-end actives. The initial $10,000 cloud/API budget is included; ongoing cloud/API costs beyond it are excluded; the modeled surplus is not net profit.

WHY THIS CAN BE A BETTER GROWTH MECHANISM A useful wardrobe and shareable outfit experience could acquire users through existing users, so organic additions scale with the installed active base instead of advertising alone. Possible loops include shared looks and friend referrals. These are candidate growth mechanisms to test; they do not expand the fixed MVP contract automatically or prove sustained organic growth. A large unpaid user base can also dilute shopping conversion. The model requires the growing audience to keep producing purchase intent; cheaper users alone do not produce $100m revenue.

TEAM AND EXECUTION Prabhjot leads product. Prabhsimran, Anthony and Joseph lead expansion and investor outreach. PSQUARE COMPANY delivers the $90k MVP and included interim support for up to one year; the separately funded Fashion Tryon team takes over earlier when ready. Retailer/feed approvals, app eligibility and reliable attribution remain unconfirmed. Broad US access first; UK/Canada/UAE launches in Year 2 and Australia in Year 3 remain conditional on local access and execution.

FUNDRAISING TIMING February 2027 is the modeled seed close, not a commitment. Begin investor discussions before launch. If funding is late, postpone hiring/acquisition or secure a bridge. Do not preserve the same growth path without its spending. Review progress against the operating requirements before spending each major tranche.

WHAT INVESTORS SHOULD UNDERSTAND This is a founder-directed target and a reverse calculation. It does not promise $114.639m, a valuation, an exit or an investor return. A 25% reduction in the organic coefficient lowers modeled Year 5 revenue to about $39.94m, even with seller fees held constant. Success depends on repeated referral growth, retention, shopping conversion and viable costs, not merely one launch spike.
