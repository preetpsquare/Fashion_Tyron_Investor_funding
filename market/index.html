Official context: the US Census Q4 2025 release estimated total US retail e-commerce at $1,233.7 billion for 2025, up 5.4%, accounting for 16.4% of retail sales [7]. That is all retail categories, not Fashion Tryon's TAM. Census and ECDB use different methods; do not combine their estimates into a precise category share.

TAM — TOTAL ADDRESSABLE MERCHANDISE OPPORTUNITY US-first reference: $241.320bn annual online fashion spend [1]. Long-term global reference: $1.330tn [6]. These are merchandise markets. They are NOT potential company revenue forecasts. We do not add speculative advertising revenue to merchandise TAM.

SAM — WHAT THE PRODUCT COULD ACTUALLY SERVE No exact verified SAM is available while retailer/feed access is unconfirmed. Use an explicit planning filter: US online fashion $241.320bn x 20% addressable catalog coverage x 50% shopper/use-case applicability = $24.132bn conditional annual merchandise SAM. Coverage means de-duplicated spend represented by accessible retailers/products; 20% is an assumption, not 20% of the retailer count. Applicability means purchases where mobile styling/visualization and available product data can help; 50% is also an assumption. Broad gender targeting does not make all fashion transactions serviceable. Sensitivity: 10% coverage x 50% applicability gives $12.066bn; 40% x 50% gives $48.264bn. Replace these filters with actual catalog coverage after agreements. At the same filters, the four-country expansion pool is $31.379bn; with Australia, $32.375bn. These are steady-state reference envelopes at 2025 values, not Year 2 sales or a forecast of market growth. Launch-month phasing is separate.

A REVENUE BRIDGE, NOT A REVENUE TAM CLAIM If all of the conditional US SAM passed through Fashion Tryon, 50% were commission-eligible, commission were 4%, and 80% survived reversals, the affiliate-equivalent pool would be $386.112m ($24.132bn x 50% x 4% x 80%). This is a sensitivity calculation using assumptions, not a plausible near-term capture forecast. Our SOM is calculated from users and purchase activity in note 03, never from an arbitrary market share.

PROJECTED SOM — UPDATED PLANNING CASE The revised Year 5 goal models $114.639m platform revenue on $3.315bn referred merchandise value. That is about 1.024% of the $323.751bn five-country reference and 10.239% of conditional five-country SAM, using frozen 2025 market values. The model requires 8.5m average monthly actives, not merely 8.5m downloads. This target does not change the researched TAM or validate market capture.

WHERE TO EXPAND Year 1: US only. Year 2 proposal: UK in November 2027; Canada in February 2028; UAE pilot in May 2028. Year 3 proposal: Australia from November 2028. Years 4–5 deepen these five markets; no revenue from unspecified countries. UK offers the largest researched expansion market of this group. Canada offers geographic proximity and scope to reuse the US experience; local pricing, language and merchant access still matter. UAE is a smaller pilot where the founders' business connections may help. Australia follows as an English-language candidate. This order is an analyst recommendation, not a researched guarantee of acquisition cost or demand. Country launches depend on funding and local partners. Retailer agreements, commissions, currency, stock and delivery data cannot simply be copied from the US.

COMPETITION AND OUR REASON TO EXIST Google Shopping: announced own-photo virtual try-on and conversational shopping in 2025 [8]. Product discovery and try-on alone are insufficient differentiation. Whering: offers wardrobe photo uploads, outfit planning and personalized daily styling; its site reports more than 10 million users [9]. That is a company-reported audience, not verified monthly active users or evidence of revenue. Retailers: established shopping relationships and product supply; Nordstrom already has a publisher affiliate channel [12]. They may be partners as well as competing destinations. Our proposed position: wardrobe-aware styling + budget-based shopping + appearance previews + confidence-based sizing + available deal/delivery comparisons in one free app. The combination is a hypothesis to test, not a patent, moat or first-to-market claim.

SELLER REVENUE EVIDENCE Amazon's US standard Associates schedule lists 4% for apparel, shoes, handbags/accessories, jewellery and watches [10]. This is the external-referral schedule, not the separate onsite creator schedule. It supports a 4% modeling anchor, not guaranteed approval or a rate for every seller/country. Yours Clothing US on Awin lists 5% for new customers, 3% for existing/default, a daily product feed and a 30-day attribution window [11]. Its stated retailer-site conversion is not applied to our entire user base. Nordstrom offers publisher commissions but the public page reviewed does not disclose a rate [12]. Its creator program mentions paid campaigns; that does not establish what Fashion Tryon could charge. No brand has committed to sponsorship rates or retailer software fees. Those amounts in note 03 are explicit future sales assumptions.
