FASHION TRYON | Investor discussion notes | 19 September 2026 All amounts USD. Updated after founder discussions; replaces earlier assumptions.

CURRENT GROWTH AMBITION Target $100–120m in annual Year 5 platform revenue. The projection model works back from that goal and produces $114.639m, conditional on 8.5m average monthly active users and the monetization requirements in note 03. This is an ambition and requirements calculation, not achieved revenue or a validated forecast. The initial $200k contribution/allocation is unchanged.

THE IDEA A free personal AI stylist for iOS and Android: learn a person's taste, style the clothes they already own, recommend complete outfits or individual items, preview supported products, compare available prices and delivery options, then send the shopper to the retailer to buy.

The intended repeat-use reason is daily styling, including outfits made from existing clothes. Shopping commissions and relevant, clearly labelled sponsorships monetize the purchase occasions. Suitable products may be recommended even when no commission is paid.

WHAT WE HAVE AGREED - Broad audience across genders; clothing, shoes, bags, jewellery and other fashion accessories in discovery. Available retailer data will determine actual catalog coverage. - Optional wardrobe/reference-image uploads and a conversational style profile. Users can start with chat and add their wardrobe later. - User-set budgets for individual items or complete outfits; users choose price versus delivery speed. - Fixed 2D try-on is acceptable. New previews target 15–30 seconds; saved previews are reused. Self-hosted generation is acceptable. Speed and quality are targets, not tested results. - Optional LiDAR body measurement/shape capture on supported devices, with photos and manual measurements as fallback. It remains in proposed scope, subject to a working prototype; no claim of measuring fat/muscle composition. - Confidence-based size advice from size charts, reviews and supplied measurements. Appearance previews are not proof of physical fit. - Try-on category coverage: deliver what can be made reliable within development. Do not promise every accessory at launch. - Free, unlimited consumer usage is the intended offer. No consumer subscriptions or paid try-on credits in these projections. - Purchases, payments and fulfillment stay on the retailer's website. Fashion Tryon earns only agreed seller fees/commissions, not the full purchase amount.

THE INITIAL $200,000 $90,000 (45%): PSQUARE COMPANY develops the MVP, targeting three months, and includes up to one year of post-launch support, bug fixes and retailer coordination. A dedicated Fashion Tryon team takes over if funded earlier. Do not count this included support again as a separate initial expense. $100,000 (50%): launch and marketing, including the media-buying arrangement reported by the founder. $10,000 (5%): initial cloud hosting and API costs. Ongoing cloud/API spending beyond this allowance is excluded from the projections. The projections are therefore not a complete company profit forecast.

TARGETS Development: three months, founder target; completion and store release are unverified. US launch: Christmas, December 25, 2026. Traction: we aim to reach 5,000 active US users by January 2027, supported by the $100,000 launch and marketing budget. Year 1 reporting: November 2026–October 2027; November includes prelaunch planning; the public launch is scheduled for Christmas. Year 2 starts November 2027.

$100,000 / 5,000 = $20 per target active user. This is a target budget ratio, not measured CAC. The agency arrangement has been reported by the founder; no signed proposal or delivered results were reviewed. The model does not treat 5,000 users as guaranteed or as 5,000 buyers.

COMPANY AND DEVELOPMENT ARRANGEMENT Fashion Tryon will be a separate company. It will own all project-specific code, designs and IP under the development agreement, subject to third-party licences. The $90,000 payment goes to PSQUARE COMPANY, a business associated with the founders; all partners should understand the payment and ownership arrangements. Incorporation and signed IP transfer have not been verified.

WHO DOES WHAT Prabhjot: product direction, development oversight. Prabhsimran, Anthony and Joseph: business expansion and investor outreach. PSQUARE COMPANY: initial MVP and included interim operations/support described above. Dedicated Fashion Tryon team: hired using the next funding round.

WHY INVEST AT THIS STAGE? The initial capital buys a working product and a test of repeat styling and shopping behaviour. Success means users return, relevant products are available, and some shopping activity becomes verified seller revenue. The current evidence is a founder plan, not demonstrated demand, revenue or secured retailer partnerships.

The initial allocation uses the full $200,000. There is no separate contingency or incorporation/admin reserve within it. PSQUARE's included support helps bridge launch to fundraising but does not fund continued paid advertising. Any other launch expenses need explicit coverage or reallocation.
