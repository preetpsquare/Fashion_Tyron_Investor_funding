FASHION TRYON | Investor discussion notes | Revised 19 September 2026 All amounts USD. Founder ambition, research and modeled requirements are distinguished.

THE NEW GOAL: $100–120 MILLION IN ANNUAL YEAR 5 REVENUE The founder wants an organic-growth path. We interpret the target as revenue earned over all of Year 5 (November 2030–October 2031), not merely a final-month annualized run rate and not merchandise value. The worked target produces $114.639m annual platform revenue.

This is a reverse-solved target scenario: what would have to happen to reach the ambition. It is not a validated forecast or a claim that organic growth is inevitable.

THE GROWTH MODEL Organic additions compound with active users independently of advertising. The model uses: Monthly actives = previous actives x continuation rate + newly activated paid users + newly activated organic users. Paid additions = paid acquisition spend / paid cost per activation. Organic additions = prior-month actives x organic activation coefficient k. Only genuinely new, activated users count; remove repeat invites, duplicate accounts and existing users. Reactivated former users are not also counted as new acquisitions.

The model anchors Jan 2027 at 5,000 active users. It then solves k to reach 50k, 300k, 1.4m and 4.2m year-end actives in Years 1–4 and 8.5m average monthly actives in Year 5. Those milestones are planning targets, not research estimates. Model precision shows the arithmetic, not forecast confidence.

Blended acquisition cost falls because more people arrive without a paid placement. Organic growth still has creator, content, community and referral-program expenses. THE YEAR 5 REVENUE BRIDGE 8.5m average MAU x 12 x 5 clicks x 5% orders/click = 25.5m referred orders. 25.5m orders x $130 = $3.315bn referred merchandise value. $3.315bn x 90% commission-eligible/attributed x 4% commission x 85% realization = $101.439m affiliate revenue. 200 average paying sponsors x $5,000/month x 12 = $12m sponsorship revenue. 200 retailer tools accounts x $500/month x 12 = $1.2m retailer-tools revenue. Total = $114.639m annual platform revenue. The $3.315bn shopping value belongs to retailers. Without sponsorship and tools, affiliate revenue alone is $101.439m under the same strong shopping assumptions. Consumer usage remains free and suitable unpaid-retailer products can still be recommended; the 90% monetized share must be achieved without suppressing useful unpaid recommendations.

THE $100–120M RANGE At the Year 5 funnel, net annual affiliate revenue per average monthly active user is $11.934. With $13.2m seller fees held fixed: $100m needs 7.273m average MAU; $110m needs 8.111m; $120m needs 8.949m. These are average active users across the whole year, not downloads or a year-end count. The worked 8.5m average produces 13.760m end-of-year MAU. Its final-month annualized revenue rate is $177.408m; that is separate from the $114.639m earned during Year 5.
